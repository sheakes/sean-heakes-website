Projects

Telescopes and instruments I have built, repaired and tested.

Cylindrical parabolic radio telescope

2024 – ongoing

I am constructing a cylindrical parabolic radio telescope to perform spectral line observations of 21 cm neutral hydrogen.

0.4 m open-truss Dobsonian

2022 – ongoing

With the help of the machinists in UBC's Physics and Astronomy Department, I am building an open-truss Dobsonian telescope around an f/4.5, 0.4 m mirror, using designs from A Practical Manual for Building Large Aperture Telescopes.

0.4 m Ritchey-Chrétien refurbishment

2022 – ongoing · UBC Astronomy Club

I am coordinating and carrying out the refurbishment of a 0.4 m Optical Guidance Systems Ritchey-Chrétien telescope for the UBC Astronomy Club and the UBC Department of Physics and Astronomy, with assistance from the Dominion Astrophysical Observatory in Victoria, BC.

Optical testing of refractor telescopes

2023 – 2024 · UBC Astronomy Club

I tested the club's refractor telescopes by double-pass autocollimation, using an optical flat and Ronchi gratings to determine their optical quality.

Drone photogrammetry of the John A. Galt telescope

January – April 2024 · Dominion Radio Astrophysical Observatory

I led a drone photogrammetry project to model the mechanical characteristics of the 26-metre John A. Galt radio telescope. I fabricated a custom drone and camera bracket, wrote a waypoint generator for drone positioning with geodetic Python packages, and analysed the datasets to model the telescope's distortions at different pointing positions.

Milky Way hydrogen Doppler model

2021 – 2022

Using Python, astronomy packages and open-source radio telescope data at the hydrogen 21 cm wavelength, I wrote a program that models the Doppler shift of hydrogen clouds in the Milky Way at various pointing directions.

Grant for a 140 mm apochromatic refractor

2021 · UBC Astronomy Club

I co-wrote a grant proposal to the UBC Alma Mater Society on behalf of the club for a new 140 mm apochromatic refractor.
