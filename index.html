Sean Heakes

Astronomer · Tinkerer · Traveller

I'm an astronomer from Vancouver, BC with a passion for radio astronomy, atmospheric modeling, and building both radio and optical telescopes.

This site collects my research, the instruments I've built and repaired, and photos from along the way. More about me.

Featured work

  • 21 cm radio telescope: a cylindrical parabolic telescope for spectral line observations of neutral hydrogen.
  • 0.4 m truss Dobsonian: an open-truss telescope built around an f/4.5 mirror in the UBC machine shop.
  • Drone photogrammetry: modelling how the 26-metre John A. Galt telescope distorts as it points.
  • Lunar brightness model: simulating the Moon's brightness temperature at 5 and 8.6 GHz.
