Research

My research interests are in radio astronomy, atmospheric modeling and signal processing.

Lunar brightness temperature model

2024 – ongoing · with Ken Tapping

I am developing a computational model to simulate the Moon's brightness temperature and solar radiative processes at 5 and 8.6 GHz, working with retired solar astronomer Ken Tapping.

Polarized beam convolution for the John A. Galt telescope

July – December 2024 · Dominion Radio Astrophysical Observatory

As an astronomy co-op student I programmed a Python pipeline that convolves a simulated polarized beam response for the 26-metre John A. Galt radio telescope with the Effelsberg-Bonn HI Survey.

I also wrote code to transform velocities from the galactic Local Standard of Rest to topocentric reference frames, interpolate large datasets and perform coordinate transforms, and produced figures intended for a series of academic papers.

Thunderbird South telescope commissioning

October 2023 – April 2024 · UBC, under Prof. Aaron Boley

As a research assistant I operated remote telescope systems, used automated data-capture shell scripts and Linux astronomy software for image analysis and tracking, and troubleshot optical systems and related hardware failures.

NRAO Synthesis Imaging Workshop

May 2024 · Socorro, New Mexico

I attended the National Radio Astronomy Observatory's Synthesis Imaging Workshop, covering interferometry and data analysis.
